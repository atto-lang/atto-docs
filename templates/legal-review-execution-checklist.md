# AttoLang — Legal Review, Governance, Equity and Foundation Compensation Checklist

**Status: NOT APPROVED / NOT EXECUTED.** This checklist is a pre-execution gate, not evidence of nonprofit recognition, shares issued, payroll authorized, or royalties owed.

## A. Entity and governance identity

- [ ] Confirm actual AttoPL Co / Atto Co legal name, form, jurisdiction and registered status; approve a valid **non-voting** equity class with counsel.
- [ ] Confirm Atto Foundation's correct legal name, nonprofit corporation registration, board composition, policies and **actual IRS 501(c)(3) status if already issued**. Do not state tax exemption as fact without evidence.
- [ ] Obtain counsel review of Foundation charitable purposes, private inurement/excess benefits and related-party transactions.
- [ ] Adopt [conflict-of-interest and compensation approval policy](../policies/foundation-conflicts-and-compensation-policy.md) through a duly authorized, **disinterested** board; save adoption minutes in restricted storage.
- [ ] Identify all interested/disqualified persons and disclose related-party ownership, including AttoPL Co interests; record recusals and an unconflicted quorum.
- [ ] Confirm authorized signatories for both entities; separate Company and Foundation approvals.

## B. Company 4% non-voting equity grant

- [ ] Review [commercial grant template](company-4pct-nonvoting-equity-grant.md).
- [ ] Attach signed, **fully diluted** capitalization table and define the denominator, option pool, conversion assumptions, exact units/shares and 4% calculation.
- [ ] Confirm corporate authority for a **non-voting** share/unit class and statutory class votes that cannot be waived.
- [ ] Document 3-month cliff, 36 monthly installments, earliest full vesting at month 39, and three substantive accepted monthly contributions.
- [ ] Before incorporating the [fork election clause](../agreements/founding-engineer-forking-and-naming-addendum.md), obtain jurisdiction-specific counsel review of competition, employment, wage, equity and IP law; confirm written grant consent, qualifying period boundaries/time zone, written evidence and disinterested review, pending-dispute handling, retention of vested shares, treatment of unvested units, and survival of the limited private-fork license. Do not apply cessation retroactively or to unrelated founder holdings.
- [ ] Confirm separate Foundation approval and preservation of earned wages, fees and enforceable royalties; no automatic cross-default or private Foundation equity.
- [ ] Address securities/tax law, entity form, grant consideration, worker classification and, **where actually applicable**, time-sensitive 83(b) election for transferred restricted property.
- [ ] Execute the definitive grant documents, record actual issuance in Company records and provide contributor-specific copies **privately**.
- [ ] Keep personal tax IDs, signatures and capitalization details out of broadly shared draft files.

## C. Foundation services and reasonable compensation

- [ ] Review [Foundation compensation agreement](../agreements/foundation-founding-contributor-compensation-and-ip-royalties.md) and confirm it gives **no Foundation shares, voting powers, default 4% royalty or donations percentage**.
- [ ] Collect a specific employment/contractor service proposal; screen actual worker classification and mandatory wages/benefits.
- [ ] Complete [independent compensation approval record](foundation-compensation-approval-record.md), with market comparables, full compensation from related entities, donor restrictions and disinterested votes.
- [ ] If work is contractor-based, complete and sign the [Foundation work order](foundation-paid-services-work-order.md) **before compensable services start**; otherwise use a compliant employment offer/payroll contract.
- [ ] Approve compensation and funding in advance; preserve signed work order and proof of payment in restricted storage.
- [ ] Create no automatic or retrospective deferred salary/residual by merely completing the Company vesting schedule.

## D. Foundation IP transactions

- [ ] Check whether the proposed licensor **actually owns** specified IP and whether prior employer/Company/Foundation assignments or third-party/open-source rights conflict.
- [ ] Confirm Foundation's genuine charitable use; disclose all related-party conflicts and obtain **disinterested** board review.
- [ ] Independently value the described license; document comparable fees and full consideration already paid.
- [ ] Complete and sign [specific IP license](foundation-specific-ip-license.md) with precise term, use, royalty base, reporting, termination and no double payment.
- [ ] No automatic 4% of Foundation donations, gross revenue, grants, surplus or net earnings. No perpetual residual just because of founding title.
- [ ] Review Form 1023/990/Schedule L and other required reporting with counsel, where relevant.

## E. Verification, confidentiality and signatures

- [ ] Confirm AttoLang remains **proprietary**, and founding-engineer forks are private and independently named, not "Atto"/"AttoLang".
- [ ] Run offline test suite and exact-source CI at a verified source Git tree; if the source-org Actions budget is unavailable, use an authorized **private** alternate runner with **matching blob and tree SHAs**, no cross-org tokens and no merging of the temporary mirror.
- [ ] Obtain independent nonprofit, securities, tax and employment counsel review **before** executing instruments.
- [ ] Store completed forms, contributor names, compensation comparables, IP title documents, cap tables, signed documents and board minutes in access-controlled legal record storage, **not** in the general repository.
- [ ] Log final legal entity names, final approved versions, signers, dates and counsel sign-off privately; **do not represent draft Markdown as a completed grant or salary authorization**.
