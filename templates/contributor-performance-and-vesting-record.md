## Schedule and calculation

- **Three-month initial cliff:** Months 1–3 must each qualify; **zero vested** at the end of month 3.
- **36 monthly installments:** From the first qualifying month after cliff, vest **1/36 of original Company grant** for each accepted qualifying monthly period; earliest completion **month 39**, subject to misses/pauses.
- **Three qualifying contributions per month** subject to documented quality, security, substantive value and acceptance. A few mechanical edits or unverified AI output cannot be split into artificial milestones.
- Calculation for uninterrupted months after the cliff: `vested_fraction = min(36, max(0, qualifying_months_after_cliff)) / 36`. If cliff fails or vesting is paused, no installment accrues until the applicable cure/qualification rules are met; never invent backdated vesting.
- At full qualifying vesting, **100% of the originally granted 4% award** is earned (before authorized dilution). This is not a perpetual percentage after later financings.
- Unit rounding, repurchase/forfeiture and regulatory exceptions are governed by signed definitive equity instruments, not this tracking sheet.
